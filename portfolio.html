MY CREATIVE PRACTICE

A collection of ideas and projects.

My creative work focuses on visual storytelling through photography, graphic design and publication design. I enjoy exploring different creative mediums and finding ways to use them together to communicate ideas and tell stories. I'm especially interested in how images, colors and layouts can influence the way people see and understand the world around them.

Through photography, I like capturing genuine moments and creating visual stories that reflect the people, places and experiences around me. With graphic and publication design, I take those same creative ideas and bring them together through thoughtful layouts, typography and composition. Whether I'm working on a single photograph or designing a multi-page publication, I pay close attention to how each element works together and how the overall design communicates its message.

My goal is to create work that feels visually engaging, thoughtful and meaningful. I want to combine photography and design in ways that tell stories, share information and connect with people through visual experiences.
